01 — AboutHow I work

I make complex products feel obvious.

Most of my work has been in dense B2B software — telecom CRM, billing, analytics, fintech — the kind of product where the hard part is not how a screen looks but how much a person has to hold in their head to get through it.

I work end to end: framing the problem with engineering and product, mapping the flow, building the system it will scale on, then prototyping until the decision is obvious. I ship in code-adjacent tools and read the codebase, so what I hand over is buildable rather than aspirational. Details matter to me because they decide how a product feels on the thousandth use, not the first.
